## 1. Non-negotiable rule (restated)

No inclusion rule, estimand, prior, threshold, missing-data rule, sensitivity analysis, stopping rule or reporting rule may be relaxed after inspecting whether it improves a result. Every entry below records which results were visible when it was written. All original frequentist (F) and Bayesian (B) results of the historical analysis were visible for every entry in this file. The scope amendment A33 of 10 September 2026 was made after the strict pool sizes were computed and seen: under the registered version 2.0 restriction as applied by A24 the strict point-prevalence set is k = 0 and the strict IPS set is k = 1, and the expected pools under A33 (k = 6 prevalence, k = 3 trials) were also known. A33 is therefore post hoc, it is reported as post hoc wherever it is relied upon, and the registered restriction is retained and reported as a labelled comparison analysis rather than replaced.
