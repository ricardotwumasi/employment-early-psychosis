## Decisions

1. **Review flow.** Under A33 (and the A42 adoption of their results), Drake 2013 (X03) and Porteous 2007 (X18) are included studies, and Clarke 2023 (X02; online 2022; internal identifier `CLARKE_2022`) is an included companion report of Killackey 2019 that adds no study or cohort. Their historical full-text exclusion decisions remain unchanged in `data/review/full_text_exclusions.csv` and are reported as reversed, not deleted. The current disposition layer is `data/review/review_disposition_2026-10-18.csv`.
2. **Vocational-exposure sensitivity (specification of 1 September 2026, lines 150 to 154).** Completed on the A33 point-prevalence primary set, not dropped. A cohort is excluded only when the source confirms that every participant was offered a vocational intervention (`all_offered`). Service availability conditional on the participant's wish is not a universal offer; receipt is not offer; unknown is not none, and cohorts classified `some_offered`, `no_offer_reported` or `unclear` are retained. Classifications are recorded with source pages in `data/review/release_vocational_exposure.csv`. The model, priors, counts and denominators are those of the primary analysis. The period set is not restricted.
3. **Candidate 3 and the coauthor draft.** A provisional release candidate 3 may be fitted, and a coauthor draft circulated, without search recovery. For this limited purpose this supersedes the roadmap's requirement (plan revision 1.9, Section 0.3) that a re-run of the search precede candidate 3. Gate 3 is not passed: the 17 unscreened records, the difference of eight identified records and the two unaccounted reports remain permanent historical uncertainty, and no replacement search was undertaken.
4. **Explicitly unresolved.** Search completeness; independent (second human) appraisal; submission readiness (AG's and GT's written approvals, PROSPERO version 3, GT's independent rebuild, certainty assessment on the release set, PRISMA checklist, exact-file approval).
