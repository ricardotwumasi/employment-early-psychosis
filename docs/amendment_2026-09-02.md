## 1. Purpose and binding chronology

This amendment records every change to eligibility, estimands, models, priors, thresholds, missing-data rules, sensitivity analyses, appraisal tools, certainty assessment, reporting rules and terminology since the review was registered. For each change it states the date, the rule before and after, whether the change is a defect correction or a scientific change, the reason, which results were visible when the change was decided, and which outputs are affected.

The chronology that every document must state consistently is as follows.

1. PROSPERO version 1.0 (26 May 2026) planned a narrative synthesis with EPHPP and JBI appraisal and a certainty judgement. It specified no pooling model.
2. An original frequentist random-effects meta-analysis (metafor, REML, Knapp-Hartung) was conducted for the MSc dissertation of Yanan Li (files dated 28 August 2026). Meta-analysis of any kind is a deviation from the registered synthesis method. This analysis is called the **original frequentist analysis**. It is not called registered.
3. The original frequentist results were known before the Bayesian analysis was specified.
4. The Bayesian specification was written and approved by the research team on 1 September 2026 (archived verbatim as `docs/analysis_specification_2026-09-01.txt`, SHA-256 `d6928e505182cce9135b1ce6ecef3106f7d0550e36c932a275d90edc5e338fee`). The team confirms that no Bayesian numerical output was generated before that approval. Filesystem timestamps are consistent with this (specification last modified 1 September 2026 at 23:55; earliest cached fit 2 September 2026 at 00:09) but cannot prove it. The Bayesian analysis is called **pre-specified within the research team before any Bayesian outputs were generated and subsequently registered by amendment**. Once the PROSPERO revision is public, **Bayesian analysis registered by amendment** is acceptable if this chronology appears in the same section.
5. Bayesian results were available before the PROSPERO major revision was prepared and submitted on 2 September 2026. The Bayesian analysis is therefore never described as prospectively registered in PROSPERO.
6. Both analysis families are reported in full. The Bayesian analysis is the primary inferential framework for the updated manuscript; the original frequentist analysis is retained as a transparent comparator.

## 2. Non-negotiable rule

No inclusion rule, estimand, prior, threshold, missing-data rule, sensitivity analysis, stopping rule or reporting rule has been, or may be, relaxed after inspecting whether it improves a result. The authors confirm that no entry below was selected because it produced a more favourable result. Any further scientifically necessary change must be added to this table, dated, classified, approved and made before the next inferential refit.
