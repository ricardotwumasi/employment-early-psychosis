These worksheets use the nine domains of the [JBI 2017 prevalence checklist](https://jbi.global/sites/default/files/2019-05/JBI_Critical_Appraisal-Checklist_for_Prevalence_Studies2017_0.pdf), with its Yes, No, Unclear and Not applicable responses. The target is employment among the review's broader A33 early-psychosis population under the care conditions studied. Sample-frame judgements therefore distinguish recruitment into a selected cohort from representativeness of that broader target. If the authors instead define the target narrowly as the selected programme population, record that change and reconsider the frame judgements consistently. Do not total the Yes responses or translate them into Strong, Moderate or Weak.

Porteous is an explicitly adapted application of those domains to descriptive employment-attainment proportions over variable calendar windows, not a standard point-prevalence assessment. It supplies no controlled intervention effect. RT’s approval adopts this instrument version and the stated Porteous adaptation for these three assessments.

Only source findings directly relevant to the extracted employment results are appraised here. A clear numerator is not evidence that missing outcomes are unbiased, an RCT label is not evidence of representative sampling for prevalence, and programme fidelity is not a study risk-of-bias rating.

There were 146 randomised participants, 145 after one exclusion for missing QoL data, and 100 complete follow-up cases included in the clustering analysis (printed pp. 771–772). Thus 100/146 = 68.5% of randomised participants contribute to this extracted proportion. This is not necessarily the number with ascertainable employment irrespective of QoL completeness. ## Porteous 2007

Source: [Implementing Evidence-Based Employment Services in New Zealand for Young Adults with Psychosis](</Users/k1812261/claude_code/employment_vocational_functioning_meta/manuscript/student_responses/Excluded/Porteous 2007 Implementing Evidence-Based Employment Services in New Zealand for Young Adults with Psychosis.pdf>). British Journal of Occupational Therapy 70(12):521–526. SHA-256 `2d936427b2174395d4815587bceb6de533467baf58d8a3f68a1a9618d042c195`.

Results: employment attained during two programme contract windows, 36/100 during June 2002–May 2004 and 58/125 during June 2004–May 2006. Table 2, printed p. 524, PDF p. 4, gives full-time plus part-time employment, separately from education. The 49% and 59% headlines combine employment and education and are not the employment-only numerators. The windows may share participants, and time from each participant's service entry varies. Exposure observation for the separate sensitivity: both windows concern an IPS programme. This affects the interpretation of these standalone results but does not remove the report from the review. A sensitivity that changes pooled membership does not change whether an eligible report is included in the systematic review.
