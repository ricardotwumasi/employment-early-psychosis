# Review-record files

Working evidence for Phase 3 of `NEXT_VERSION_PLAN_v1.1.md`. These files record what the source documents say; they do not repair or reconcile anything. Values were extracted programmatically on 2 September 2026 (PDF first pages with PyMuPDF; Word tables with python-docx and pandoc) and checked by the statistical and release lead.

| File | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `source_registry.csv` | One row per PDF in the workspace `literature/` folder (31 rows after the 6 September 2026 addition of Bond, Drake and Becker 2026 and its correction): print year, journal, DOI, role in the analysis, and online-first versus print-year notes. `pmid_or_pmcid` is `not_found` throughout because no PubMed identifier appears in the PDF text | To be extended to one row per included report (45 studies) as PDFs or DOIs are confirmed, and to one row per extracted result with page or table sources |
| `prisma_flow_comparison.csv` | The two dissertations' PRISMA flows side by side with an arithmetic check at each stage; column `team_agreed_2026_09_05` records the identification and duplicate counts (2,541 and 891) that YL reported on 5 September 2026 as jointly agreed with HK; column `kaplankiran_reported_2026_09_10` records the fuller flow HK reported on 10 September 2026 (2,549 identified, 891 duplicates, 1,641 screened, 1,569 excluded, 72 sought, 25 exclusions, 45 included), with the unresolved 2,549-versus-2,541 and 72-minus-25-versus-45 discrepancies carried in the comment column | Awaiting the authoritative screening export from HK; downstream boxes not derivable; no count has been repaired |
| `student_evidence_log.md` | Dated log of requests to and replies from HK and YL (facts and initials only), with verification status and open items; HK's 10 September 2026 reply is recorded as paraphrased rows in Section 2 (amendment A34), and a Section 4 records four label collisions between HK's exclusion table and the 45 included studies | Maintained by RT |
| `population_eligibility.csv` | Population-eligibility audit of the 23 extracted reports and the Jäckel 2025 candidate against PROSPERO version 2.0, with page-referenced quotations. Columns `decision_a24_strict` and `decision_a33` (added 10 September 2026) record, respectively, eligibility for the strict primary set under amendment A24 (derived from `conflict_with_prospero_v2`) and eligibility under the broader scope amendment A33 (approved by RT, AG and GT on 10 September 2026; written approval outstanding), derived by applying the A33 population and duration rule to the existing extracted columns. `decision`, `amendment_ref` and `rationale` were updated to record the A33 outcome, with the A24 strict reading preserved separately in `decision_a24_strict` | Checked by RT against the PDFs on 10 September 2026 (every row); A33 status still awaits AG/GT written confirmation |
| `candidate_results.csv` | Inventory of every candidate employment or vocational result in the same 24 reports (construct, basis, window, denominator type, comparator, ascertainment, page) mapped to the current `result_id` where one exists (amendment A25, with measurement-rule corrections under A36) | Checked by RT against the PDFs on 10 September 2026 (every row; `checker` was already RT throughout); no selection made |
| `full_text_exclusions.csv` | The 25 full-text exclusions HK supplied on 10 September 2026 as a Word table (`exclusion_table.docx`, converted with python-docx), one row per exclusion with the citation, decision and reason as supplied, a `reason_class` classification, any label collision with the 45 included studies, and whether the study needs A33 rescreening | Supplied by HK, 10 September 2026; reasons not independently re-checked against source PDFs |
| `a33_universe_worklist.csv` | Worklist of studies not yet in the primary meta-analysis that need A33 population and A25 result status checks: the 22 `included_studies_45.csv` rows with `in_meta_analysis` not "yes", Drake et al. (2013) from the exclusion table (A38 population no longer excludes it), and a placeholder for the 17 AIM Review "red" records not yet screened. Three studies with a local PDF in `literature/` (`chan2020`, `hegelstad2017`, `jackel2025`) have a determined A33 population status and A25 result status from the first pages of the PDF; the rest are "pending retrieval" | Draft of 10 September 2026 |
| `novelty_crosswalk.md` | Comparison of this review's two pools with Bond, Drake and Becker 2026 and the two earlier Bond reviews (amendment A28) | Draft |
| `correction_ledger.csv` | Input ledger for the fourteen source-linked corrections (A6) with class, date, approver and disposition; `R/00_clean_data.R` reads it and regenerates `data/derived/corrections.csv` | Authoritative input from 6 September 2026 |
| `reconciliation_dispositions.csv` | Disposition for each row flagged in `data/derived/reconciliation.csv`; the cleaner fails if a flagged row has no disposition | Authoritative input |
| `result_crosswalk.csv` | Written by `R/00_clean_data.R`: raw row id, bibliographic report id, registry key, DOI, cohort id, historical `result_id`, corrected `result_id_current` and `result_uid` for every extracted result | Generated; do not edit by hand |
| `primary_estimand_compatibility.csv` | The A5 primary-estimand compatibility flag and reason per prevalence study; the cleaner asserts agreement with the legacy `population_fep` column | Authoritative input |
| `ephpp_two_source_check.md` | Programmatic comparison of the EPHPP component tables in the two dissertations (43 quantitative studies, zero differences), the global-rating rule check, and the absence of the Blinding component and JBI items 6 and 7 from both | Blinding and JBI items 6 and 7 requested from HK |

Conventions: `study_key` is the lower-case first-author surname plus the print year, matching `manuscript/sr_data/included_studies_45.csv`; the print year with the DOI is the citable identity, so Dudley is 2014, Hegelstad (Job- and schoolprescription) is 2019, Tapfumaneyi is 2015, Erickson is 2021 and Bond (IPS for young adults) is 2016, whatever year the file name carries. The derived analysis tables in `data/derived/` use upper-case identifiers with the same print years.

`exclusion_table.docx` is HK's source Word document for the 25 full-text exclusions, supplied 10 September 2026; `full_text_exclusions.csv` is its machine-readable conversion and is the file other tables and worklists should reference.

Check-date normalisation, 10 September 2026: `population_eligibility.csv` and `candidate_results.csv` both had `checker` set to RT throughout from RT's Excel review of 10 September 2026, but `check_date` was a spreadsheet fill-down running from 10/09/2026 to later calendar dates one row at a time, not real per-row check dates. Both files' `check_date` columns were reset to `2026-09-10` on every row to remove this artefact.
